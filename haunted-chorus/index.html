The Haunted Chorus

Giving physical characters a voice through custom software, digital fabrication, and projection-mapped performance.

Demonstration video: two physical character busts used as projection surfaces. Video asset will appear here once published to the site.

From physical sculpture to performed character

This independent show-technology build combines 3D-printed character busts with a custom digital performance workflow. Two physical figures become the canvas for a Haunted Mansion–inspired singing-bust presentation, connecting fabrication, software, and projection alignment in one installation.

01 / Digital fabrication

3D-printed character busts provide detailed, dimensional surfaces that give the projected imagery physical presence.

02 / ChorusForge

Custom software developed for the performance-production workflow, connecting the digital content to the final character presentation.

03 / Tesselume Mapper

Custom projection-mapping software used to align imagery with each sculpted surface. The mapping workflow supports calibrated, prewarped content for standalone playback.

Engineering perspective

The challenge is not simply playing video. It is coordinating a digital performance with real three-dimensional geometry, where projector placement, surface shape, and visual alignment all affect the final illusion. Building the physical objects and the software tools together provides control over the entire workflow.

An independent, Haunted Mansion–inspired fan project; not an official Disney production.
