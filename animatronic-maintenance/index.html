Methodology / Signal Math

What we read, how we transform it, why it matters, and what maintenance decision it supports.

Core rule: raw values are rarely actionable alone. We compare each actuator against the expected behavior for the same move, pose, direction, speed, acceleration, temperature and configuration. All equations below are engineering feature definitions for the proposed platform and require validation on the actual hardware.
RAW MOTION

Commanded position · qcmd(t)

qcmd(t)

Source: TwinCAT motion setpoint / drive target position.

Reasoning: Defines intended pose and trajectory phase. It is the reference for motion residuals and pose-conditioned cable analysis.

Action: If an apparent anomaly follows a changed command, review animation/configuration before replacing hardware.

RAW MOTION

Actual position · qact(t)

ep(t) = qact(t) − qcmd(t)

Source: motor/load encoder feedback.

Reasoning: Measures physical response. Sudden discontinuities can indicate feedback-path faults; gradual direction-dependent changes can indicate compliance/backlash.

Action: Electrical path inspection for discontinuities with normal effort; mechanical inspection for load-dependent residual growth.

DERIVED

Velocity / acceleration / jerk

v = dq/dt   a = dv/dt   j = da/dt

Prefer drive/TwinCAT values when available. Numerical derivatives must be filtered because differentiation amplifies noise.

Reasoning: Normalizes effort by dynamic demand and identifies high-reversal/high-jerk cable stress.

Action: High-jerk-only anomalies point toward restraint, whip, routing or dynamic mechanical loading.

SERVO

Following error

e(t)=qcmd(t)−qact(t)
RMS = √[(1/N) Σ e²]

Also calculate peak, p95/p99, phase-specific energy and waveform similarity.

Reasoning: Detects subtle tracking changes below the hard fault threshold.

Action: Error + current rise → load/drag; impulse + feedback invalidity → encoder/cable; oscillatory settle → tuning/compliance/resonance.

EFFORT MODEL

Current residual

Î = f(q,v,a,dir,T,move,config)
ΔI = Imeas − Î

Source: Elmo measured current or equivalent drive estimate.

Reasoning: Raw current changes with commanded motion. The residual asks whether the motor is working harder than expected for the same motion.

Action: Persistent positive ΔI supports friction, binding, brake drag, alignment or cable/hose interference inspection.

EFFORT MODEL

Torque residual

T̂ = f(q,v,a,dir,Tmotor,move)
ΔT = Tmeas − T̂

Use correctly scaled drive torque. Do not count current-derived torque as independent evidence from current.

Action: Growing ΔT across matched cycles strengthens a mechanical-load hypothesis.

MOVE FEATURE

Motion effort / energy

I²t = ∫ I(t)² dt
Eeffort = ∫ |I(t)| dt

Reasoning: Condenses the electrical work required to execute a defined move and can be trended move-to-move.

Action: Rising effort with the same trajectory generates a WATCH/INSPECT condition depending on persistence and corroborating signals.

DYNAMIC RESPONSE

Settling time

ts = first t after move end where |e(t)| < ε for Δthold

ε and hold duration are validated per actuator/move rather than universal constants.

Reasoning: Increased settling can reveal compliance, looseness, friction or tuning changes.

Action: Combine with vibration and effort before choosing mechanical vs controls inspection.

CABLE

Feedback event rate

λ = Nevents / Nopportunities × 1000

Opportunities are valid move executions or traversals—not hours.

Reasoning: Normalization prevents a high-use actuator from looking worse simply because it moves more often.

Action: Trend λ and its slope; rising normalized rate creates early cable/feedback evidence.

CABLE LOCALIZATION

Pose-conditioned anomaly density

λ(θbin) = Nevents,bin / Ntraversals,bin × 1000

Reasoning: A fractured conductor may open only when a joint bends the harness through a specific geometry.

Action: A repeatable hotspot maps the task to the harness flex segment stressed at that pose.

CABLE EXPOSURE

Flex exposure index

F = Σ(w₁|Δθ| + w₂R + w₃|v| + w₄|j| + w₅Zpose + w₆T)

R = direction reversal event. Zpose weights validated high-strain zones. Weights begin as relative engineering factors and are calibrated against field/bench data.

Action: Prioritize high-exposure harnesses; do not call this remaining life until validated against failures.

CABLE FATIGUE

Direction changes / rainflow cycles

R = count(sign(vk) ≠ sign(vk−1))

For calibrated bend-angle surrogates, rainflow counting can estimate cyclic damage more meaningfully than raw show counts.

Action: Supports PM interval research and harness-family comparisons.

DIAGNOSTIC LOOP

Sacrificial conductor resistance

Rcomp = Rmeas / [1 + α(T−T₀)]
ΔR = Rcomp − Rbaseline

Temperature compensation is required because copper resistance changes with temperature.

Reasoning: A witness conductor may expose flex damage without injecting diagnostics into operational encoder/safety conductors.

Action: Rising ΔR or intermittent continuity generates inspection only after correlation with the actual harness is experimentally validated.

HOMING

Home repeatability

σhome = √[Σ(qhome,i−q̄home)²/(N−1)]

Also track search duration, retries, edge quality and current/torque during search.

Action: Rising σ with clean electrical edges → mechanical/reference inspection; noisy edges → sensor/wiring inspection.

VIBRATION

RMS / crest factor / kurtosis

aRMS=√[(1/N)Σa²]
CF = |a|peak/aRMS

Kurtosis adds sensitivity to impulsive behavior. These are calculated on comparable move windows.

Action: Rising vibration + effort residual strengthens a mechanical looseness/bearing/reducer hypothesis.

VIBRATION FFT

Spectral band energy

X[k]=DFT{x[n]}
Eband=Σk∈band|X[k]|²

Sampling and anti-alias filtering must support the frequency range of interest.

Reasoning: New harmonics or band-energy growth can reveal resonance, looseness or rotating/transmission defects.

Action: Use as corroboration; a spectral peak alone is not a unique diagnosis.

NETWORK

EtherCAT error rate

rECAT = Δ(counter) / Δt

Track CRC/RX/lost-link/WKC deltas with counter-reset awareness and network topology.

Action: Motion-correlated link errors point to the moving network segment; downstream coincidence points to a common upstream link.

FUSION

Cross-channel coincidence

C = count(events from channels sharing asset path within ±Δt)

Reasoning: Several channels failing together are stronger evidence of a common harness, connector, supply or network segment than independent device failures.

Action: Use the physical topology graph to localize the common segment.

HEALTH

Health index

H = 100 − g(z₁,z₂,…, persistence, confidence)

z-values are normalized deviations from qualified healthy baselines. The production form is versioned and validated; it is not an arbitrary average of raw sensors.

Action: Health is a navigation/priority summary. Every score must expose the evidence that moved it.

ML

Anomaly / cause models

x = [ΔI, ΔT, eRMS, ts, λ, F, EFFT, …]

PCA / Isolation Forest learn unusual behavior from known-good feature vectors. XGBoost/Random Forest later rank failure domains from confirmed repair labels.

Action: Models contribute evidence and ranking; an engineering-controlled action engine selects the maintenance task and preserves UNKNOWN.

Platform Architecture

From synchronized figure telemetry to a verified maintenance outcome.

01 · AcquireTwinCAT + Elmo position, velocity, current, torque, feedback, states, EtherCAT, I/O and optional sensors.
02 · ContextFigure, axis, animation, move, pose, direction, harness, connector, maintenance revision.
03 · FeaturesResiduals, settling, event rates, pose density, flex exposure, spectra, cross-channel coincidence.
04 · InferExpected behavior, anomaly detection, cause ranking and degradation trends.
05 · ActGenerate a targeted inspection or verification task with evidence and physical location.
06 · VerifyRecord the actual finding, repair, measurements and post-repair signature recovery.

Runtime deployment

TwinCAT handles bounded acquisition and optional validated inference. A local service handles historian, Python training, heavier analytics and the browser dashboard.

Safety boundary

TwinSAFE, drive protection, hard limits, interlocks and deterministic control remain authoritative. The ML platform observes, explains, trends and recommends.
